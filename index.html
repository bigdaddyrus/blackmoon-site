The Sound. 声音

A sanctuary for roots music and physical record culture — a room built around the record, where every needle drop is hand-chosen and every track plays end to end.

Tucked behind a black hutong door, the room is tuned for low-volume listening: a carefully built analog signal path, acoustic treatment across the brick walls of a 200-year-old courtyard, and a wax-only rotation pulled from a decade of digging across China, New York, California, and Tokyo.

Come early for quiet sets and a slow drink, stay for selectors deep into the night on weekends.

Core curation

Hip-hop. Soul / Funk. Jazz. City Pop. — the soulful, dusty warmth of classic beat tapes and underground pressings.

The Space. 环境

A restored siheyuan courtyard behind a grey hutong door — brick, tungsten, vinyl, and very little else.

Twenty-five seats across the main listening room and a lounge. A vinyl wall stretching the full length behind the bar, and a single warm-glow filament behind the booth.

The Plate. 小菜

A real kitchen, not a snack menu — slow-cooked, fire-finished, plated to sit alongside a long pour and a longer record.

We are not audiophiles.

We are not obsessed with vinyl as a mere medium,
nor do we chase the nostalgic warmth of analog sound.

Our devotion belongs to the music itself—
and the profound histories, text, and cultures woven behind it.

Yet, spinning a record—
a tangible artifact traveling through time—
unmistakably infuses a space with an inexplicable magic.

It creates a beautiful crease in time,
bringing the air of a bygone era into the present moment.

At this precise coordinates, the act of playing music
transcends utility, and enters the realm of poetry and ritual.

In a world of weightless, frictionless streaming,
we choose friction.

We choose the conscious weight of a slower pace.

We do not worship the equipment; we honor the story.
We do not possess the technology; we inhabit the ritual.

— Blackmoon · 黑月 · 2025
